Collector Sync - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What data the extension accesses

Collector Sync requests only the Google Drive drive.appdata scope. This lets it create, read and update files in a hidden application data folder that belongs to this extension inside your own Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

How the data is used

The items you collect with the extension are saved to that hidden folder so they stay in sync between your own browsers. The data is used for no other purpose.

Sharing and storage

Your data stays in your own Google account. It is not sent to any server run by the developer, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

Removing your data

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the hidden app data from Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Collector Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
